## Mission
Mary's House Services provides safety, support and independence for women and children affected by domestic and family violence in Sydney, Australia.

## Services
- **Crisis accommodation / refuge** — a safe place for women and children escaping domestic violence.
- **Outreach & case management** — wrap-around support including legal, financial, housing and counselling navigation.
- **Children's wellbeing programs** — trauma-informed programs supporting children's recovery and development.
- **Community education & advocacy** — raising awareness and shifting attitudes that enable violence.

## Beneficiaries
Women and children in the greater Sydney region who have experienced or are at risk of domestic and family violence. Services are trauma-informed, culturally sensitive, and inclusive of LGBTQIA+ relationships.

- Children's programs (specialist therapists, materials, outings).
- Outreach worker capacity expansion.
- Capital improvements to safe accommodation.
- Technology and data systems for case management.
